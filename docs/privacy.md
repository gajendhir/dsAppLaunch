# Privacy Policy — Data Spec Application Launcher

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

Data Spec Application Launcher ("dsAppLaunch") is a desktop program that shows
a grid of the programs you choose and starts them. This page explains what it
does and does not do with your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** dsAppLaunch does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal
information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no
telemetry, and it makes no network connections of its own.

## What is stored on your computer

- The list of programs you add (names, file paths, descriptions, colours and
  ordering) is saved in a plain text file, `apps.json`, in your own user profile
  (`%AppData%\Data Spec\dsAppLaunch`). It never leaves your computer and is
  removed when you delete that folder.
- The app reads the program files you list in order to show their icon and the
  date they were last changed, and starts them when you click **Launch**. It
  does not read their contents or send any information about them anywhere.

## Links

The footer link may open a web page (https://www.dataspec.info) in your default
browser when you click it. That is a normal browser visit governed by that
site's own policy.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children and collects
no information from anyone.
